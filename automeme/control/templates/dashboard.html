How it works

  1. Finds fresh, fast-rising memes & cute animals from Reddit every few minutes.
  2. Screens each through 16 safety checks & removes duplicates.
  3. Scores them (growth speed, taste, past performance) and keeps the best.
  4. Posts to @icr8meme on schedule — memes & animals alternating.
  5. Learns from likes/reposts to improve over time.
